# Privacy Policy – PeakCut

**Last updated: November 21, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

PeakCut is a tool designed to help creators identify highlights, generate video clips, and manage content from livestreams.

This Privacy Policy explains how PeakCut handles information when users connect third-party platforms, including TikTok.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, PeakCut may access:

- Basic account information provided by connected platforms.
- Account identifiers and profile information.
- Video content and information needed to publish videos.
- Authorization tokens required to connect accounts.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

- Connect authorized social media accounts.
- Identify and create video clips.
- Prepare and publish videos when authorized by the user.
- Maintain the application's functionality.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

PeakCut is designed to run locally on the user's computer. Information processed by the application may be stored locally, depending on the configuration.

Users should protect their computers and any account authorization credentials.

## 5. Third-Party Services

PeakCut may connect to third-party services, including TikTok. Their use is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

PeakCut does not control how third-party platforms process information.

## 6. Data Sharing

PeakCut does not sell users' personal information.

Information is shared with third-party platforms only when necessary to provide features requested and authorized by the user.

## 7. Data Deletion

Users may disconnect their accounts and delete locally stored application data.

Users may also revoke PeakCut's access through the connected platform's account settings.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PeakCut is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.
